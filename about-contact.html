🏥 About RuralCare

RuralCare is a digital healthcare platform designed specifically for rural areas, connecting patients with doctors through an easy-to-use appointment system.

Our mission is to make quality healthcare accessible to everyone, regardless of their location.

✨ Our Features

👨‍⚕️

Doctor Booking

Find and book appointments with verified doctors in your area.

💊

Medicine Delivery

Get prescribed medicines delivered to your doorstep.

🚨

Emergency SOS

Quick access to emergency services and nearby hospitals.

📱

Offline Access

Works even with poor internet connectivity in rural areas.

🎯 Our Vision

To become India's leading rural healthcare platform, serving 10,000+ villages by 2025.
